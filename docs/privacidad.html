Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · This is a translation; if in doubt, the Spanish version prevails.

Recuérdame is a reminder app made by one person. This page explains what data it uses, what for and what you can do about it. The short version: only what is needed for the app to work and sync between your devices is stored; there are no ads, no analytics, and no data is sold.

Controller

Fernando Munguía Hoyo, Cantabria (Spain). Contact: info@lamaltesa.dev.

What data is processed

  • Your account: your name, your e-mail address and a user identifier. If you sign in with Google, your Google password is never received. If you sign in with your e-mail, there is no password: each time a one-time code is sent to you through Amazon (Cognito and SES).
  • What you create in the app: reminders (name, description, frequency, dates, photos you attach, address), categories, places (name, Wi-Fi network and, if you add them, a location and a radius) and the name you choose to display.
  • Location you save: if you tap “use my location” on a place or a reminder, the coordinates at that moment are saved with it (with their approximate address). Only when you ask for it.
  • Friends and shared reminders: the name and e-mail of your friends, and who marked a shared reminder as done.
  • Invitation links: a random code along with your name, until someone uses it, you cancel it or it expires (after 7 days).
  • Notifications: a device identifier to send you updates about shared reminders.
  • Suggestions: the text you send from the app, the app version and a short code derived from your user (not your name or e-mail).

Data that never leaves your phone

  • Background location: if you turn on arrival alerts, the app checks on the phone itself, also in the background, whether you are connected to a place's Wi-Fi network or inside its area. That location is not sent to or stored on any server; only the location you choose to save is, as explained above.

What it is used for

Only to provide the service you ask for: storing and syncing your reminders, notifying you, sharing reminders with your friends and handling your suggestions. The legal basis is the performance of the service you accept by using the app.

Who else sees it

  • Your friends see your name and e-mail, and the reminders you share with them.
  • Providers that host or transmit the data, acting as processors:
    • Amazon Web Services: servers and photo storage, in the Stockholm region (EU).
    • Google: sign-in and notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
    • Expo: forwarding of shared-reminder notifications.
    • GitHub: suggestions are published as issues in a public repository, and the app checks there for new versions.
    Some of these providers may process data outside the EU under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Data is not sold or given to anyone else, nor used for advertising.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. From the app (menu → User settings → Delete account) you can delete your account and all your data at any time; it is deleted immediately, and in the history of reminders other people shared with you your name is replaced with “Deleted user”. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your account by e-mail. Suggestions already published on GitHub contain no personal data and can be removed on request.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, or ask for a copy by writing to info@lamaltesa.dev. If you think your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Children

The app is not intended for children under 14.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date and, if the change is significant, you will be told in the app.
