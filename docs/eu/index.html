Deskargatu Recuérdame

Aplikazioa proba-fasean dago eta .apk fitxategi batetik zuzenean instalatzen da.

Deskargatu azken bertsioa
  1. Ireki esteka mugikorretik eta deskargatu .apk fitxategia.
  2. Ireki. Androidek baimena eskatuko dizu jatorri ezezaguneko aplikazioak instalatzeko: eman.
  3. Sartu Googlerekin edo sortu kontu bat zure posta elektronikoarekin, eta onartu jakinarazpenak.

Ez duzu Google konturik behar: edozein posta elektronikorekin sor dezakezu bat. Aurrerago, Google Playn ere bai.

Berritasunak

Bertsio bakoitzak dakarrena (ingelesez). Aplikazioak abisatzen dizu bertsio berri bat dagoenean.

Version 2.3.0

Oct 21, 2026
  • Your region's holidays: in Settings > Holidays you pick your autonomous community (or Ceuta or Melilla) and its holidays are marked by themselves every year, with their name and in a different colour from the national ones. The local holidays you add yourself can now carry a name (the patron saint, the fair…), have a colour of their own and show under the calendar. Saturdays and Sundays are shaded so they are easier to tell apart. Each region's dates change a little from year to year: you can remove or add the ones that do not match.
  • Place-only reminders: in the Place section of the form, “Only when I get there” removes the date and time: the reminder alerts you when you arrive at the place. Until then it does not get in the way or show as overdue: it has its own “Waiting” button in the status bar (before Completed), appears at the end of the pending ones and under the place in Places. It is not in the calendar, the widgets or the daily summary. It needs arrival alerts to be on.
  • Animated loading screen: when the app opens, the logo breathes and some dots hop instead of staying still.
  • More useful pop-ups at the top: the messages shown when you add, complete or snooze a reminder are dismissed by swiping them aside and open the reminder when tapped (after adding one by voice or with the quick line, they open its edit form).
  • Dictation better understood: phrases such as “add a reminder to call Nacho on Friday at 3” no longer keep “add a reminder to” in the name. It understands “add/create/set a reminder to/for…” (and the equivalents in Spanish, Catalan, Galician, French and German).

Version 2.2.0

Oct 20, 2026
  • Fixed a crash in the Week and Month views: people who had used the app before 2.1.0 could see it close suddenly when switching on the national holidays (Settings > Holidays) or opening the week and month views. The saved holidays are now always completed, so it no longer fails.
  • Scrolling widgets: the “Reminders” and “Agenda” widgets can be scrolled with a finger, so you see all your reminders and not only the ones that fit. Tapping one opens it, as before.
  • First day of the week: in Account you can choose whether the week starts on Monday or Sunday, whatever the language. It applies to the week and month views, Holidays and the list's groups (this week, next week…). By default it follows the language.

Version 2.1.0

Oct 19, 2026
  • National holidays of Spain: the calendar (week and month views) now marks each year's national holidays by itself —New Year's Day, Epiphany, Good Friday, Labour Day, Assumption, National Day, All Saints, Constitution Day, Immaculate Conception and Christmas— with their name under the calendar and a more visible highlight. In Settings > Holidays you can switch them off, remove a single one or keep adding your region's or city's own.
  • Large icon on alerts: each alert of a reminder with a category now shows the category emoji on the right, on a circle in the category colour, so you can recognise it at a glance (it is no longer repeated in front of the title).
  • PDF export: reminders are listed by date (pending first, the soonest on top; then the completed ones) and the file is named “recuerdame-date.pdf”, like the JSON.

Version 2.0.0

Oct 19, 2026
  • Minimum version: if a very old version stops being compatible with the service, the app says so and takes you to update instead of failing.
  • Tags: add free-form tags (up to 10) to a reminder from the new “Tags” section of the form, with suggestions from the ones you already used. They show on the card and the detail screen, can be filtered by tapping them above the list (several at once) and are found by search. People a reminder is shared with see them and can change them.
  • Quick add and dictation: the list has a line to type (or dictate with the microphone) “call the dentist tomorrow at 6pm #health”. The app understands dates, times, “in 2 hours”, repeats (“every day”, “every Monday”), #tags and #categories, with no internet or AI, and shows it before saving; if it does not understand the time, it opens the form so you choose. It also applies to short text shared from another app, and the new-reminder form has a dictate button. The microphone sits next to the “+”; after dictating, a card shows what was heard (editable) and what was understood, with icons; “Add” saves with “Undo”, and if the time is missing it opens the form. It also understands “I want you to remind me…”, “every Monday”, “every morning”, “every weekend”, “in half an hour” and “next week”.
  • Alert check: a new screen in Settings that checks why an alert might not sound (notification permission, silenced channel, exact alarms, battery saver, location for place alerts, shared-alert registration), with a button to the right Android setting, tips for Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei and Oppo, and a test alert in 5 seconds.
  • Weekly summary: a “Your week” screen with what you did and snoozed in the last 7 days, your streak and the reminder that resists you most, with comments in a light tone. It opens by itself the first time you open the app since Sunday, and the comments are 100 different phrases that change every week. Optionally, a weekly alert (day and time of your choice) that also opens it; it is off until you turn it on in Account.
  • Export my data: in Account you can download everything you have (reminders, lists, categories, places, tags and history) as a JSON file, or your reminders and lists as a readable PDF, and save or share it wherever you like.
  • More recognisable alerts: the daily and weekly summary, friends, shared reminders, arrival at a place and new-version alerts carry an emoji telling what they are, besides the title.

Version 1.15.0

Oct 18, 2026
  • Expected repeats on the calendar: in the week and month views, repeating reminders also appear, faded and with the original’s icon, on the days they are expected to come up again. Tapping a day lists them as “Expected”; they can’t be completed or snoozed until they arrive.
  • Lists shared directly: a list can be shared with a friend without any reminder. They can edit it (check, add, remove and reorder items) and leave it, and whoever created it is told. Only the creator can delete it. The list shows who it is shared with and which people come through a reminder.

Version 1.14.2

Oct 18, 2026
  • Form in sections: when you create or edit a reminder you only see the name and the date; the rest — Repeat, Category, Place, Description, Attachments, Lists and Snooze — sits in folding blocks that summarise what is chosen. One opens at a time and the open one stands out in colour.
  • Several attachments: a reminder now takes up to 10 photos and PDF files (#26). Pick several photos from the gallery at once or use the camera, and PDFs from the phone’s files.   • Repeats with an end (#22): a repeating reminder can end on a date or after a number of times. When it reaches the end, the last “Done” moves it to “Completed”. The repeat and its end show on the card and the detail screen.
  • Calendar: in the month view, two reminders on the same day with the same category show their icon twice, one per reminder (#25). With more than two, a “+N” is added.
  • Notice on decline: if someone rejects a reminder you shared with them, you now get a notification (before, it just stopped being shared without telling you).
