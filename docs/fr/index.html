Des rappels sans calendrier

Pas quand.
Mais tous les combien.

Certaines choses n'ont pas de date, mais elles ont un rythme : arroser les plantes, changer le filtre à eau, vérifier les pneus, appeler vos amis. Dites à Recuérdame tous les combien vous les faites et il vous préviendra quand ce sera à nouveau le moment.

Télécharger pour Android Gratuit · Sans publicité · Android 7.0 ou plus récent

Un calendrier dit

« Le 1er de chaque mois. »

Même si vous l'avez fait hier. Même si vous avez deux semaines de retard.

Recuérdame dit

« 30 jours après la dernière fois. »

Quand vous le marquez comme fait, le compte repart de ce moment-là. Si vous le reportez, la fois suivante est décalée aussi.

Comment ça marche

  1. 1

    Dites-lui tous les combien

    Tous les 3 jours, toutes les 2 semaines, tous les 6 mois… en minutes, heures, jours, semaines, mois ou années.

  2. 2

    Il vous prévient au bon moment

    Une notification avec Fait et Reporter. Ou, si vous préférez, quand vous arrivez à la maison ou au travail.

  3. 3

    Vous le faites, et c'est reparti

    Vous marquez que c'est fait et l'intervalle repart de ce moment-là. Pas de dates qui s'accumulent ni de rappels en retard.

Tout ce qu'il vous faut, rien de plus

📍

En arrivant à la maison

Associez un rappel à un lieu et il vous préviendra à votre arrivée, si c'est le moment. Il détecte le Wi-Fi de la maison ou du travail, il consomme donc très peu de batterie.

👥

Tâches partagées

Invitez votre conjoint ou votre famille avec un lien. N'importe qui peut le marquer comme fait et le compte repart pour tout le monde.

🎨

Catégories en couleur

Maison, voiture, santé, plantes, animaux… chacune avec son icône et sa couleur, dans la liste et dans les notifications.

📷

Photo et lieu

Joignez une photo du filtre à acheter ou l'adresse du garage. Les photos sont enregistrées compressées pour prendre peu de place.

⏰

Reportez sans culpabiliser

Pas maintenant ? Reportez de 15 minutes ou de 2 jours. Si une tâche vous résiste, l'app vous encourage et vous propose même de la rayer du doigt pour décompresser.

📱

Widgets

Gardez sous les yeux, sur l'écran d'accueil, combien de tâches sont en retard ou reportées, et lesquelles.

🎉

Un plaisir à faire

Quand vous marquez quelque chose comme fait : des confettis et un message d'encouragement. Et la notification ne disparaît pas tant que vous ne l'avez pas fait ou reporté.

☁️

Dans le cloud

Connectez-vous avec Google ou simplement votre e-mail (sans mot de passe : vous recevez un code), et vos rappels vous suivent si vous changez de téléphone. Vous pouvez aussi les consulter hors ligne.

Téléchargez Recuérdame

L'app est en phase de test et s'installe directement à partir d'un fichier .apk.

Télécharger la dernière version
  1. Ouvrez le lien depuis votre téléphone et téléchargez le fichier .apk.
  2. Ouvrez-le. Android vous demandera l'autorisation d'installer des applis inconnues : accordez-la.
  3. Connectez-vous avec Google ou créez un compte avec votre e-mail, et acceptez les notifications.

Pas besoin de compte Google : vous pouvez en créer un avec n'importe quel e-mail. Bientôt aussi sur Google Play.

Nouveautés

Ce qu'apporte chaque version (en anglais). L'app vous prévient quand il y en a une nouvelle.

Version 1.9.0

Oct 7, 2026
  • Clearer whether a reminder happens once or repeats: when creating it you choose between Just once and Repeats, each with an explanation. If it repeats, you see when it will remind you again after marking it as done, and Default snooze explains that snoozing doesn't change how often it repeats.
  • Fixed-date reminders: when you choose Repeats you also choose how: On fixed dates (every day at 21:00, every Monday, the 1st of each month, every 29 October…), where doing it late doesn't delay the next reminder, or From when you do it (as before: the count starts when you mark it as done). The list and the reminder page show when it's due (“Every week, on Monday at 15:00”).
  • Delete several completed reminders at once: in Completed, tap Select (or press and hold one) to pick several, or Delete all to empty the list. Reminders shared with you are only removed from your list.
  • Help: a new menu section with the most common questions: the two kinds of reminder, the difference between snoozing and repeating, arrival alerts, sharing and what to do if reminders don't arrive.
  • Fixed: the notification's quick button (for example, +10 minutes) did nothing with the app closed.
  • A shorter menu: Send a suggestion, the website and what's new move to About.

Version 1.8.1

Oct 6, 2026
  • Fixed: shared-reminder notifications (“X did it”) didn't arrive with the app closed: the phone couldn't register to receive them (the server registers it now) and, with the app closed, the message arrived but nothing handled it.
  • More reliable arrival alerts with the app closed: the Wi-Fi name is read straight from the connection. If your phone's location is turned off, Places tells you, because without it the app can't tell when you arrive.
  • When allowing location from Places, the app explains first what it needs it for.
  • User settings → Technical data shows whether the phone is registered for notifications and what places last detected, to help diagnose problems.
  • The app no longer declares the permission to draw over other apps, which it didn't use.

Version 1.8.0

Oct 6, 2026
  • Ready for Google Play: the app changes its internal identifier, so Android treats it as a new app. Uninstall the previous version first and sign in again: your reminders are in the cloud and nothing is lost. If you don't uninstall it, you'll have both and get every alert twice.
  • When you delete your account, your name goes too from the history of reminders other people shared with you: it shows “Deleted user” instead.

Version 1.7.0

Oct 5, 2026
  • Undo: when you mark something done or snooze it, the message at the top has an Undo button for a few seconds. If the reminder was overdue, it alerts you again.
  • History: each reminder's page shows when it was done and who did it, very handy for shared ones (“did you water them or did I?”).
  • Search the list by name, description, address, category or place, without worrying about accents.
  • Snooze without asking: when swiping, press and hold Snooze to snooze straight away for the reminder's default time (or 10 minutes if it has none).
  • Snooze from the notification: notifications have a new button with the default time (for example, +10 minutes) that snoozes without opening the app. The Snooze… button still lets you choose the time.
  • Daily summary (optional): one notification a day, at the time you choose, with what you have for that day, overdue ones included. Turn it on in User settings, on each phone.
  • Snooze again from the list: a snoozed reminder that isn't due yet can also be snoozed again by swiping it left, like overdue ones.

Version 1.6.0

Oct 5, 2026
  • Arrival alerts, only if you want them: they come turned off. Before turning them on, the app explains why it needs your location (for example, to remind you to water the plants when you get home and not at the supermarket). If you don't turn them on, everything works the same and reminders with a place alert you at their time. Turn them on and off in Places. If you had already given the permission, they stay on.
  • Up to 5 suggestions a day per person, with a friendly notice if you reach the limit.
  • Safer shared reminders: whoever receives a shared reminder can change what to do and when, but no longer who owns it or who it's shared with.
  • New iPhone icon (the app is still Android-only, for now).

Version 1.5.0

Oct 5, 2026
  • One account per e-mail: if you create your account with your e-mail and later sign in with Google using the same address (or the other way round, for new accounts), it's the same account, with the same reminders. The first time they're joined you may have to tap “Continue with Google” twice. Existing Google accounts stay as they were.
  • If you ask for a sign-in code for an e-mail that has no account, the app tells you (before, you waited for an e-mail that never came).
  • Fixed: after creating an account with your e-mail and entering the code, it showed “password is required to signIn” instead of signing in.
  • E-mails in your language: the codes to activate your account and to sign in arrive in the app's language (Spanish, English, Catalan, Galician, Basque, French or German).

Version 1.4.0

Oct 4, 2026
  • Sign in with your e-mail, no passwords: you no longer need a Google account. Create an account with your name and e-mail (you get a code to activate it) and, every time you sign in, the app sends a new code to your e-mail. If you already sign in with Google, keep doing so: they would be separate accounts.
  • Home-screen widgets, in the app's colours: a big one with the counters, the first overdue and snoozed reminders (tap one to open it) and a + button to create another, and a small one with four buttons in two rows: new, snoozed, overdue and pending. Long-press the home screen → Widgets → Recuérdame.
  • Celebrate what you do: when you mark something done there's confetti, the phone vibrates and an encouraging message appears (and a special one if you had snoozed it many times).
  • Encouragement when snoozing: if you snooze the same task more than 3 times, the app cheers you on with a message for its category (home, car, health, plants…); from the sixth time, with a warmer one.
  • Scribble and breathe: from the sixth snooze (and then every three) the app offers an anti-stress mini-game: the task appears several times on the screen and you scribble over it with your finger until it's gone. Then you can mark it done, snooze it or play again.
  • Notifications don't get lost: a reminder notification now only goes away when you complete, snooze or delete it (you or someone you share it with). Tapping it opens the reminder without removing it, and after restarting the phone or updating the app, the overdue ones come back, silently.
  • Done always asks for confirmation in a panel at the bottom, like snooze: when swiping, on the reminder's page, from the notification button and in “Do it anyway”.
  • Fixed: swiping a reminder right and tapping Done did nothing.
  • Fixed: when swiping a card, bits of the buttons underneath showed through.
  • Fixed: you couldn't complete or unsnooze a reminder a friend shared with you (“Unauthorized on [snoozedUntil]”).
  • Editing a completed reminder and giving it a future date makes it active again and it alerts you (shared ones too).
  • In “About”, a link to lamaltesa.dev, the website with all my apps, and a confession in the bio. The mother-in-law gives up her spot in the introduction to the washing machine.

Version 1.3.1

Oct 3, 2026
  • Thicker borders on the reminder list cards, so the status colours stand out.
  • Fixed: on some accounts you couldn't share your own reminders (“only whoever created the reminder can share it”).
  • When sharing a reminder with a photo, place or address, the app warns that they're shared too (and that, with a place, your friend will get the alert when they're there).

Version 1.3.0

Oct 3, 2026
  • Invite friends with a link: in Friends → Invite a friend, share a link on WhatsApp, Telegram or however you like. When they open it, the other person sees your invitation in the app and accepts you. Each link works once and expires in 7 days; you can cancel it from Friends.
  • If the link doesn't open the app, its code can be pasted or typed in “Did someone send you an invitation?”.
  • Reminder page: tapping a reminder in the list (or its notification) opens a page with what to do and the Done and Snooze buttons; to change it, the pencil at the top.
  • Done and Snooze each lead to their own screen, with just that option. The notification buttons go straight there: “Done” marks it at once and “Snooze” opens the snooze screen (before, it opened the one with both options).
  • New signature: the app is now signed with its own key (safer, and required for Google Play). So, to move to this version you have to uninstall the previous one and install this one, and sign in again; your reminders are on the server and aren't lost.
  • You no longer invite by e-mail or from the phone's contacts, so the app stops asking for permission to read contacts.

Version 1.2.0

Oct 3, 2026
  • Final server: the app moves to the production server. You have to sign in again, and reminders, categories, places and friends from the test versions are not kept.
  • Your friends appear with their name (the one they have in the app or their Google account's), not just their e-mail.
  • Inviting friends and sharing reminders now works: a server bug made all of these fail.
  • Swipe to act: right to mark as done (it asks for confirmation and the countdown starts again from that moment, even if it wasn't due yet) and, on overdue ones, left to snooze. The first time there's an overdue one, the list shows you both gestures.
  • Places without being there: a place's area can be set by searching for its address.
  • In the menu, the logo and “Recuérdame” take you to the list, and your name to the user settings.
  • Fixed: when opening an alert from the notification and snoozing it, the OK button did nothing.
  • Status colours throughout the app: overdue in red, snoozed in amber and completed in green, the same on the Done and Snooze buttons, cards, filters, the reminder screen and the alert.
  • A single place field when creating a reminder: pick one of your places or tap ➕ New to create one; when you save it you're back at the reminder with the place already chosen.
  • “Alert on arriving at” becomes “Alert when I'm at”, with a clearer explanation.
  • The photo is added right below the description, and the form shows which fields are required (*) and which are optional.
  • Status filters in the list: pending, overdue, snoozed and completed (to see the ones you've done that don't repeat), each with its colour, icon and count. They always fit the phone's width.
  • Your friends have their own little animal and colour, like categories. Tap a friend to choose others (only you see them).
  • The same with categories: ➕ New opens the new-category screen and, when you come back, leaves it selected without losing what you had typed.
