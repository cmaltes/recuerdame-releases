Delete your account

Recuérdame, by Fernando Munguía Hoyo · Last updated: 6 October 2026

You can delete your Recuérdame account and all your data whenever you want, in two ways.

From the app

  1. Open Recuérdame and sign in.
  2. Open the side menu and tap User settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

It is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Without the app, by e-mail

If you no longer have the app installed, write to info@lamaltesa.dev from the address you sign in to Recuérdame with, with the subject “Delete my account”. Your account and your data will be deleted within 30 days at most, and you will get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, e-mail address and user identifier.
  • Your reminders, with their photos, addresses and history. The ones you shared with other people disappear for them too.
  • Your categories and places (name, Wi-Fi network, location and radius).
  • Your friends, requests and invitation links, and your devices' notification identifiers.

What is kept

  • In reminders other people shared with you, the history still shows when it was done, but says “Deleted user” instead of your name.
  • Suggestions you sent, published on GitHub without your name or e-mail. They are removed on request.

No copies of your data are kept after deleting it.

More information in the privacy policy.
